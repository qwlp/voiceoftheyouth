Voice Of the Youth Team

Chan Sochhen (Ms.) - Founder & President

Remarks from the Founder 

I am thrilled to extend my warmest greetings to each and every one of you. As the founder of this incredible organization, it brings me immense joy to share our story with you.

At the tender age of 15, fueled by passion and driven by a vision, I embarked on a journey to create a platform that would amplify the voices of the youth. The path was anything but easy. Challenges seemed insurmountable, obstacles daunting, but our determination never wavered.

Today, standing here and looking back at the arduous yet fulfilling journey, I can proudly say that we've created something extraordinary. Voice Of the Youth stands tall as a beacon of empowerment, a sanctuary for those passionate about debate and public speaking.

Throughout our voyage, we've witnessed the transformative power of dialogue and eloquence. We've seen young minds blossom, their confidence soar, and their voices resonate in rooms where they once felt unheard.

The impact we've made, the lives we've touched - it's a testament to the resilience and dedication of our vibrant community. Together, we've not only honed our speaking skills but also fostered a culture of respect, understanding, and empathy.

As we continue on this remarkable journey, I invite you all to be a part of this incredible movement. Join us in championing the art of debate and public speaking, in empowering the voices that yearn to be heard, and in shaping a future where every opinion matters.

Thank you for being here, thank you for believing in our cause, and thank you for being a part of Voice Of the Youth. Together, let's amplify the voices that will change the world.
